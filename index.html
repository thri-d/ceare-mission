The 15-Minute Evaluation

A client needs results from their summer internship program, fast. Your team has one dataset, one mission, and an AI teammate. Let's see what you can build.

📨 The request

FromProgram Director, Desert Bloom Career PathwaysToCEARE Evaluation TeamSubjectBoard meeting this afternoon URGENT

Hello CEARE team,

Our board meets this afternoon, and they want to see how our summer internship went. I have attached our data from 48 interns across four sites.

Could you send us four things: a findings section for our report, a dashboard we can show the board, a short summary in English and Spanish for our families, and ideas for what we should ask next year?

I know this is short notice. Is 15 minutes possible?

Thank you!

👥 Your team, your roles

Decide in the first 30 seconds. Every role matters.

🧑‍✈️

Pilot

Shares their screen, uploads the data, and types into the AI.

🧭

Navigator

Reads the mission card, and tells the Pilot how to improve the prompt or the output.

🔍

Auditor

Gives the dataset and the final output to a second AI and asks it to check the work. Reports back to the team.

🎯 The missions

Your team has one mission. Together, the four teams deliver everything the client asked for.

Team 1

📝 The Report Writers

Write the Findings section of the evaluation report.

Tool: Gemini (upload the Excel file)

Pilot prompt
You are an evaluation report writer. Using the attached data from the Desert Bloom Career Pathways summer internship, write the Findings section of an evaluation report for the program's board. Use these headings: 4.1 Participants 4.2 Change in Career Self-Efficacy 4.3 Results by Site 4.4 Job Offers 4.5 What Interns Said Include numbers and clear, professional sentences. About 400 words.
Team 2

📊 The Dashboard Builders

Build an interactive dashboard the board can explore.

Tool: Gemini → Tools → Canvas (upload the Excel file)

Pilot prompt
Using the attached data from the Desert Bloom Career Pathways summer internship, build an interactive dashboard for the program's board. Show: - Key numbers at the top: number of interns, average career self-efficacy before and after, and job offer rate - A chart comparing the four sites - Three highlighted quotes from interns Make it modern, colorful, and easy to read.
Team 3

🗣️ The Community Voice

Turn the interns' comments into a summary for families.

Tool: Gemini (upload the Excel file)

Pilot prompt
Read the Final_Comment column in the attached data from the Desert Bloom Career Pathways summer internship. 1. Group the comments into 4 to 5 themes and count how many comments fit each theme. 2. Choose one powerful quote for each theme. 3. Write a warm, one-page summary for families and community members, in both English and Spanish, that shares what the interns experienced.
Team 4

🔭 The Next Round

Plan what the program should ask next year.

Tool: Gemini (upload the Excel file)

Pilot prompt
Using the attached data from the Desert Bloom Career Pathways summer internship: 1. Tell me which site and which parts of the program worked best, and why. 2. Based on that, design 6 survey questions and 4 interview questions for next year's evaluation that will help us understand what made the program successful.
All teams

🔍 Auditor prompt

Use a different AI from the Pilot (for example, the UA GenAI tool, ChatGPT, or Claude). Attach the same Excel file, then paste the Pilot's output below this prompt.

I am attaching a dataset and an AI-generated output that was created from it. Please review the output and tell me if anything should be corrected before we send it to our client. [Paste the Pilot's output here]
