Team roles

Every role matters. If you prefer not to type prompts, choose Navigator or Auditor.

Pilot

Shares their screen, uploads the data, and types into the AI.

Navigator

Reads the mission, and helps the Pilot improve the prompt and the result.

Auditor

Gives the data and the final result to a second AI, and asks it to check the work.

Your mission

Select your team. Each team creates one deliverable. Together, the four teams answer the whole request.

Pilot prompt
You are an evaluation report writer. Using the attached data from the Desert Bloom Career Pathways summer internship, write the Findings section of an evaluation report for the program's board.

Use these headings:
4.1 Participants
4.2 Change in Career Self-Efficacy
4.3 Results by Site
4.4 Job Offers
4.5 What Interns Said

Include numbers and clear, professional sentences. About 400 words.

Team 2

The Dashboard Builders

Build an interactive dashboard that the board can explore.

  1. Open Gemini and start a new chat.
  2. Click Tools, then choose Canvas.
  3. Upload the Excel file.
  4. Copy the prompt below, paste it, and send. Open the preview to see your dashboard.
Pilot prompt
Using the attached data from the Desert Bloom Career Pathways summer internship, build an interactive dashboard for the program's board.

Show:
- Key numbers at the top: number of interns, average career self-efficacy before and after, and job offer rate
- A chart comparing the four sites
- Three highlighted quotes from interns

Make it modern, colorful, and easy to read.

Team 3

The Community Voice

Turn the interns' comments into a summary for families.

  1. Open Gemini and start a new chat.
  2. Upload the Excel file.
  3. Copy the prompt below, paste it, and send.

Pilot prompt
Read the Final_Comment column in the attached data from the Desert Bloom Career Pathways summer internship.

1. Group the comments into 4 to 5 themes and count how many comments fit each theme.
2. Choose one powerful quote for each theme.
3. Write a warm, one-page summary for families and community members, in both English and Spanish, that shares what the interns experienced.
